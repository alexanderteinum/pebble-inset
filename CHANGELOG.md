# Changelog

## 0.6.1 – 2026-10-28

- Refine time alignment and battery margins across all models.
- Match battery margins to the curved screen edge on Round and Round 2.
- Remove the optical vertical offset for a centered layout.

## 0.6.0 – 2026-10-27

- Always show Bluetooth and Quiet Time icons, dimming inactive states to match the battery indicator.

## 0.5.0 – 2026-10-27

- Increase time and date font sizes on Pebble Time 2 and Round 2.
- Add more space between status icons on Time 2 and Round 2.

## 0.4.1 – 2026-10-23

- Vibrate on both phone connection and disconnection when enabled, respecting Quiet Time.
- Rename the setting to “Vibrate on connection change”.

## 0.4.0 – 2026-10-22

- Use Open Sans Medium for time and date.
- Add optional vibration on phone disconnect, off by default and silent during Quiet Time.

## 0.3.0 – 2026-10-22

- Show Bluetooth disconnect and Quiet Time icons beside the date.

## 0.2.1 – 2026-10-21

- Use light mode by default.

## 0.2.0 – 2026-01-20

- Add dark mode.

## 0.1.0 – 2026-01-19

- Initial release.
